2. Traced into sprites, 64 pixels tall

Cut out, downsampled with area averaging, quantised to the Mega Drive's colour levels and 14 colours per character shared across every frame, a one-pixel dark outline added. These are real sprite data (rows of characters plus a palette), not pictures: the walk cycle is 2.4 KB gzipped.

5. Recommendation

Gaz is E, at 64 pixels tall, on a 320x224 screen. Bitmap Brothers shading, a Human Traffic cast, the whole thing at Streets of Rage scale. The brief's 12x16 was right for a 256-wide 1-bit game and this is not that any more.

The pipeline: generate in this style with the fashion spelled out, trace with the tool (now part of the repo), fix by hand where the check says so, keep the reference image beside the sprite. Backgrounds the same way: a generated Bitmap-style room at 320x224, traced to a 32-colour indexed bitmap, which the maths says is 15 to 25 KB gzipped per screen. A level page stays under the 128 KB gate with room for the light system.

What it costs: each generated image is a Figma AI credit on the Food plan. The parade used nine. A full cast and twelve backgrounds with a retry each is about forty.
