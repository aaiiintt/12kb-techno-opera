15KB Techno Opera

15KB Techno Opera is a short interactive story that fits in 15 kilobytes.

Audio synthesis & choreography9.8 KB
Musical score & act storyboards2.6 KB
Photonic bloom & Swiss typography1.5 KB
HTML stage & markup0.5 KB
Total transfer footprint14.5 KB (fits in 15 KB)

About this project

Large AI systems make it easier to build small software. We can use them to make tools that serve one purpose, one person, or a small group. The more of these small tools we make, the less we might rely on large systems in the future. Maybe it's time for some less is more, digitally too.

I wanted to see if I could tell a story in a few kilobytes. This was my attempt. It ended up being a short opera about technology. Played out using dots.

Repo: github.com/aaiiintt/15kb-techno-opera/
