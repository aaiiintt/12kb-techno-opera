# Script — 15KB Techno Opera

A sixty-second timeline of Silicon Valley told on a 9x9 grid of dots.
Hue only, no names, no logos. Cold open. Loops.

| # | Sequence | Secs | Dot trick | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dial-up | 0-6 | CRT raster scans the grid on row by row, phosphor green | Modem handshake from noise and two tones |
| 2 | Four dots | 6-12 | Four Google-coloured dots bounce at centre, then claim rows and rank the grid by brightness | First C major, tenor motif |

| 4 | Contagion | 15-23 | Blue spreads cell to cell, heart glyph pulses, red notification pops accelerate | Chorus builds, pings in the scale |
| 5 | Scroll | 23-30 | Whole grid scrolls upward, every dot entrains to one pulse | Heartbeat becomes 120 BPM kick |

| 7 | The scrape | 36-44 | All colour flows inward and sums to white at centre, rest goes dark | Every voice converges on one note |
| 8 | Slop | 44-52 | White radiates out, all dots turn beige, hallucination flickers, dots swell past cells | Unison, kick at 140, chromatic climb |
| 9 | Pop | 52-57 | One kick, silence, fast decay from the edges in | Silence, then C1 pedal |
| 10 | Loop | 57-60 | Two warm dots remain, new gold dot born one cell left, restart | One heartbeat |

## Rules

- Under 15 KB gzipped, single file, zero dependencies.
- Brands as hue only: FB blue 214, Google 217/4/45/145, Amazon orange 36, AI white, slop beige.
- Surtitles rejected: fully abstract.
