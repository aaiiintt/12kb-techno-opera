# Story cards v3

Written against the third visual guide. Each card follows one hero disc through the opera's own acts. Per act: one lighting state, one mass move, one soloist move, and the sounds that light them. Lights are named from the palette; nothing else. Music blocks carry over from v2 unchanged where noted.

---

### CARMEN (French, tragedy)

- **Logline.** A soldier's heart is taken by a woman who will not be kept, and he cannot let her go.
- **Hero.** José, a gold disc, tenor. We follow him.
- **Others.** Carmen, a coral disc, soprano: she moves freely, never on the grid's straight lines. The crowd is the field in bulb and coral.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background black throughout; one cut to coral at the knife.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `5 4 3 2 1`, rhythm `x-x.x-x.`, the Habanera's sinking line over a tonic pedal. D Phrygian, tempo 72, ritardando into the last chord. Carmen sings first; José answers in canon; José takes her motif when he is lost to her; retrograde when he cannot undo it; augment on the stab. Room bright and wide, closing as the story tightens, near-silent for the knife.
- **Kawaii.** José has a heartbeat you can see: his disc pulses on a soft kick. Carmen's flower is a single sparkle that lands on him. When he is in love his disc is a little bigger.

**I. The guard.** (intro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and dusk.
- Soloist: José at the centre, lit only by his heartbeat, slow.
- Mass: none. The field is dusk.
- Cue: none.

**II. The flower.** (bars 1 to 4)
- Light: coral enters. Gold, coral.
- Soloist: Carmen hops in from the left edge on the beat, singing the motif; her disc follows every note. She circles José, one cell away, never touching.
- Mass: a slow scan in dusk-coral along the bottom row, one cell per beat, the arp's pedal made visible.
- End of act: she throws the flower. One sparkle arcs to José; his disc pops and grows a little. His heartbeat quickens.
- Cue: L'AMOUR.

**III. The crowd.** (bars 5 to 8)
- Light: bulb and coral. The crowd cheers for the toreador.
- Soloist: José answers her motif in canon, a beat behind. He shrinks a step each bar as the crowd grows.
- Mass: a chase in bulb around the grid's outer ring, faster each bar, lit by the arp and the hats.
- Cue: TORÉADOR!

**IV. Lost to her.** (bars 9 to 12)
- Light: coral only. Gold is gone from the field; only José is gold.
- Soloist: José sings Carmen's motif on his own tenor, the change of voice. His disc follows it.
- Mass: fillCentre in coral from Carmen's cell, ring by ring on each planed chord, until José is surrounded.
- Cue: JE T'AIME.

**V. Libre.** (bars 13 to 15)
- Light: coral and bulb.
- Soloist: Carmen breaks away, hopping to the far corner. The coral field follows her: every lit cell decays except those near her.
- Mass: an explode in coral from Carmen's corner on the crescendo, the one big burst of the piece.
- Cue: LIBRE.

**VI. The knife.** (bar 16)
- Light: black, then a cut to coral background on the stab.
- One full bar of silence: everything dusk, even José.
- The stab: a single flash on the dry chord, the background cuts to coral for one beat, closeUp on Carmen's disc as it burns out on the augmented retrograde.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: ADIEU.

**VII. Alone.** (outro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and dusk. Background black.
- Soloist: José at the centre, where he began. The pedal note holds; his heartbeat returns, slower.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: CARMEN...
- **Turnaround.** The last state is the first: José alone at the centre in dusk, heartbeat slow. The loop is seamless.

**Running time.** About 60 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** José's heartbeat alone; Carmen circling with the scan; the chase at its fastest; the coral fill surrounding José; the explode; the closeUp on the burning disc.

### PAGLIACCI (Italian, tragedy)

- **Logline.** A clown paints on a smile that keeps cracking until it splits for good.
- **Hero.** Canio, a gold disc, tenor. We follow him: the story is his mask slipping, and only a disc lit by his own heartbeat can show that from the inside.
- **Others.** Nedda, a sakura disc, soprano: the beloved truth he can't hold. The troupe is the field in bulb.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background black throughout; one cut to coral at the crack.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `1 -3 -4 2 1`, rhythm `x-x.xx-.`, "Vesti la giubba"'s falling sixth, a sob that never quite arrives back up. A minor, tempo 96, easing into every deceptive cadence then snapping back. Canio sings it first, painting the smile; fragment for the chorus; change voice as Nedda answers bright, Canio answers back minor; diminish as the mask cracks; augment, slow and funereal, as it splits. Room dry and close, narrowing as the mask strains, opening wet at the crack.
- **Kawaii.** Canio has a small hiccup-hop whenever he tests his own smile, comic and a little sad. It comes with a soft downward chirp, like a slide whistle giving up.

**I. The paint.** (intro, about 5 s)
- Light: gold and dusk.
- Soloist: Canio hops once at centre, the hiccup-hop, testing his face.
- Mass: none. The field is dusk.
- Cue: none.

**II. Ridi.** (build)
- Light: gold and bulb.
- Soloist: Canio pops brighter, the smile going on.
- Mass: a flash across the bulb field, once, the troupe's laugh.
- Cue: RIDI!

**III. Pagliaccio.** (the mask held)
- Light: gold and sakura. Nedda enters.
- Soloist: Canio pulses, his heartbeat under the held pose, fighting to keep it steady.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: PAGLIACCIO!

**IV. Nedda's truth.** (the wrong laugh)
- Light: sakura only; gold shrinks small beside it.
- Soloist: Canio shrinks, the smile straining.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none — the deceptive cadence needs no cue.

**V. Finita.** (the mask breaks)
- Light: a cut to coral background; gold fades in it.
- Soloist: Canio fades.
- Mass: burn — the gold field cools cell by cell, unevenly, the mask cooling to nothing.
- Cue: FINITA.

**VI. La commedia.** (outro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and dusk. Background black.
- Soloist: Canio at centre, the hiccup-hop returns, slower.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: LA COMMEDIA.
- **Turnaround.** The last light is dusk with Canio's small hop, the same one the piece opened on. The loop is seamless.

**Running time.** About 55 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** Canio's hiccup-hop alone; the flash on RIDI!; the heartbeat held under the pose; his shrink beside Nedda's sakura; the burn eating the gold field; his fade back into the opening dusk.

### RIGOLETTO (Italian, tragedy)

- **Logline.** A jester's curse comes true when he opens a sack expecting his enemy and finds his daughter.
- **Hero.** Gilda, a sakura disc, soprano. We follow her: the court and the curse are things that happen to her, and her journey — contained, drawn out, taken — is the one worth tracking.
- **Others.** The Duke, a gold disc, tenor: careless, orbiting. Rigoletto, her father, is the bulb field itself — warm, dim, ordinary devotion, never a saturated colour of his own.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background black throughout; no cuts — the court stays bulb, gold and sakura.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `1 4 3 2 1`, rhythm `x.xx.x.x`, "La donna è mobile"'s rising fourth over a tonic–dominant rock that never settles. G major, tempo 108, a snap ritardando before the sack opens. The Duke sings it first, bright and careless; change voice as Rigoletto vows the curse low on bass; invert for Gilda's answer; retrograde under the reveal, the tune running backwards. Room bright and dry through the Duke's charm, narrowing as Rigoletto believes he's won, blowing wide open and cavernous at the sack.
- **Kawaii.** Gilda carries a tiny star on a ribbon, a faint sparkle riding her disc. A music-box chime ticks with every one of her hops.

**I. Alone.** (intro, about 5 s)
- Light: sakura and gold, the Duke's aria heard distant and dim.
- Soloist: Gilda hops once, small and contained within her own cell.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: LA DONNA.

**II. Glimpsed.** (the court)
- Light: gold and sakura.
- Soloist: Gilda grows a little, drawn toward the sound.
- Mass: orbit — the Duke's gold disc circles the court once, careless.
- Cue: MOBILE!

**III. The vow.** (Rigoletto's fear)
- Light: bulb dims low; sakura holds.
- Soloist: Gilda shrinks as her father's dread reaches her.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: MALEDIZIONE.

**IV. Taken.**
- Light: gold only; sakura flees at the edge.
- Soloist: Gilda flees toward the corner.
- Mass: orbit tightens around her cell.
- Cue: none.

**V. The sack.** (the reveal)
- Light: bulb fading, sakura pops into view where gold was expected.
- Soloist: Gilda pops into the sack's place.
- Mass: explode — the one burst of the piece, sakura outward from her cell.
- Cue: VENDETTA! then GILDA!

**VI. Alone again.** (outro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold resumes a distant, dim orbit; sakura is gone; dusk field.
- Soloist: Gilda's disc fades, one last time.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.
- **Turnaround.** The last light is the same dusk the piece woke in, the Duke's orbit already circling somewhere else — the curse resets on the next fool.

**Running time.** About 60 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** Gilda's contained hop; the Duke's orbit as she grows; her shrink under the vow; her flee as the orbit tightens; the explode at the reveal; her fade into the closing dusk.

### DIDO AND AENEAS (English, tragedy)

- **Logline.** A queen sings her grief once, over a bassline that never stops walking downward, and then she's gone.
- **Hero.** Dido, a violet disc, soprano. We follow her: the ground bass is the world's weather, but grief still needs one disc to carry it, or there is nothing to watch.
- **Others.** Aeneas, a sky disc: distance, departure, brief warmth. The court is the bulb field, the ground bass made visible beneath her.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background violet-grey throughout, deepening toward black at the lament.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `1 -7 -6 -5`, rhythm `x-x-x-x-`, the ground bass of the Lament, no leap, a descending line repeating under everything. G minor, tempo 56, the one place tempo never flexes — the breath happens through augmentation, not tempo. The ground bass states first and never stops; the vocal line enters stepwise above it; augment as it weakens, doubling in length against the unmoved bass; diminish briefly for a comfort-fragment, swallowed back in; change voice at the end as the bass migrates onto a hollow chorus timbre, outliving the singer. Room half-closed throughout, feedback climbing slowly — grief here is a slope.
- **Kawaii.** Dido carries a tiny wilting flower: her disc's edge dips very slightly on each pulse, like a droop. A soft downward breath sits under the ground bass's repeat.

**I. Alone.** (intro, about 8 s)
- Light: violet and dusk.
- Soloist: Dido pulses slow, alone at centre, the ground bass made visible in her.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.

**II. Arrival.**
- Light: violet, sky enters.
- Soloist: Dido grows a little, drawn toward the sky-light at the grid's edge.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.

**III. Departure.**
- Light: sky recedes to dusk; violet only.
- Soloist: Dido shrinks as the sky-light fades from the edge.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.

**IV. The ground holds.**
- Light: violet deepens.
- Soloist: Dido pulses again, heavier.
- Mass: snow — violet cells drift downward one row per beat, fading as they reach the bottom.
- Cue: REMEMBER ME.

**V. Grief's crest.** (the bloom)
- Light: violet floods briefly, then begins to fall.
- Soloist: Dido fades.
- Mass: decay — every lit cell fades together on an exponential curve to black.
- Cue: FORGET MY FATE.

**VI. The ground remains.** (outro, about 8 s)
- Light: from black, violet relights thin, the background matching the intro's violet-grey.
- Soloist: one last faint pulse before dusk.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.
- **Turnaround.** The last state is the first: violet-grey dusk, the ground pulse about to begin again — grief always sinking toward its next fall.

**Running time.** About 75 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** Dido's slow pulse alone; her grow toward the sky-light; her shrink as the sky recedes; the snow drifting under REMEMBER ME; the decay swallowing the grid; the last faint pulse in the returning dusk.

### THE MAGIC FLUTE (German, comedy)

- **Logline.** A prince is sent into trials by a queen's fury and comes out the other side worthy of the woman he loves.
- **Hero.** Tamino, a gold disc, tenor. Of Tamino or Pamina, we follow him: the trials are his to pass, and a hero's journey needs a disc that grows through fear into steadiness.
- **Others.** Pamina, a sakura disc: glimpsed, then rescued. The Queen of the Night is a sky force in the field; Sarastro's temple is lemon.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background black throughout; one cut to lemon at the temple.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `1 1 1 5+ 6+`, rhythm `x.x.x.x-`, "Der Hölle Rache"'s octave leap and stabbing jab before the climb. D minor under the Queen, tempo 132 dropping to 84 under Sarastro. The Queen sings the full unstable shape first; fragment as offstage stabs; transpose a third down, scared; change voice plus a major-minor flip as Sarastro answers calm in the parallel major. Room tight and bright under the Queen, opening wide and warm under Sarastro — a hard cut, not a ramp.
- **Kawaii.** Tamino carries a tiny star that trails his hops, the flute's magic made visible. Each hop plays a small ascending trill.

**I. Serpent.** (intro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and dusk.
- Soloist: Tamino flees, startled.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: DER HÖLLE.

**II. The portrait.**
- Light: gold, sakura glimpsed small.
- Soloist: Tamino grows, smitten.
- Mass: sparkle — a few cells glint around Pamina's sakura, a keepsake shimmer.
- Cue: none.

**III. The Queen's fury.**
- Light: gold and sky.
- Soloist: Tamino shrinks, small before it.
- Mass: flash — the sky field snaps to full brightness once, the fury's stab.
- Cue: RACHE!

**IV. Resolve.**
- Light: gold, sakura small.
- Soloist: Tamino grows steady, resolving to face the trials.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.

**V. The temple.** (dawn)
- Light: gold and lemon.
- Soloist: Tamino grows tall, the trial passed.
- Mass: rainbowCentre — the one rainbow of the piece, hues rotating outward from the centre in rings.
- Cue: WEISHEIT!

**VI. Together.** (outro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and sakura reunited; background black.
- Soloist: Tamino bounces once, joyful.
- Mass: sparkle, a few glints around them both.
- Cue: HELP!
- **Turnaround.** The light fades back to the same gold-and-dusk solitude that opened it, Pamina's sakura just leaving the frame — the serpent always waiting for the next Tamino.

**Running time.** About 65 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** Tamino's flight from the serpent; his grow at the portrait's sparkle; the flash under RACHE!; his steady grow before the trial; the rainbowCentre at WEISHEIT!; the reunited sparkle before the loop.

### DON GIOVANNI (Italian, dark comedy)

- **Logline.** A statue that cannot move keeps coming anyway, one heavy step at a time, until it arrives.
- **Hero.** Giovanni, a gold disc, tenor. We follow him, not the statue: dread only lands if we watch the one it's coming for, careless right up to the end.
- **Others.** The Commendatore is a sky force in the field, cold and advancing. Elvira and the rest are folded into the bulb field.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background black throughout; one cut to sky as the wall floods it.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `1 1 1 1`, same note wide spacing, rhythm `x---x---`, the Commendatore's hammer-blow chords, dread from stillness. D minor, tempo 60 for the hammer, a long held pause before the final chord. The Commendatore states it first, bare and huge; Giovanni answers the same tune fragmented into a jaunty triplet; Elvira sings it augmented, pleading, over his dance; invert once as Giovanni mocks the statue's own shape back at it, upside down, right before it costs him everything. Room cold and huge throughout; Giovanni's scenes sit brighter and drier until the statue's room floods in and overwrites them.
- **Kawaii.** Giovanni carries a little wink-sparkle that pops beside his disc on every hop, a small self-satisfied flourish, with a cheeky pizzicato pluck to match.

**I. The swagger.** (intro, about 5 s)
- Light: gold and dusk.
- Soloist: Giovanni pops, a confident little entrance.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.

**II. Viva la libertà.**
- Light: gold and sky, the statue faint at the far edge.
- Soloist: Giovanni bounces, playful.
- Mass: fillEdge begins — the far row lights sky-grey, sweeping in one step.
- Cue: VIVA LA LIBERTÀ!

**III. Chi siete.**
- Light: gold and sky.
- Soloist: Giovanni grows, mocking, unbothered.
- Mass: fillEdge advances another row; camera: shake on the hammer-blow chord.
- Cue: CHI SIETE?

**IV. Ridi.**
- Light: gold and sky.
- Soloist: Giovanni pops again, laughing it off.
- Mass: fillEdge advances again.
- Cue: RIDI!

**V. Pentiti.** (the wall arrives)
- Light: sky floods the grid; gold dims.
- Soloist: Giovanni shrinks, then fades as it closes on him.
- Mass: fillEdge completes, sky sweeping the last rows; camera: shake, hard, on the final chord.
- Cue: PENTITI!

**VI. Silence.** (outro, about 8 s)
- Light: dusk returns; sky recedes to a fixed dim row at the far edge; gold returns faint at centre.
- Soloist: one last flicker where Giovanni stood, then gone.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.
- **Turnaround.** The same dusk as the intro, the statue's row already fixed at the far edge, waiting for the next fool.

**Running time.** About 60 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** Giovanni's confident pop entrance; the sky wall's first advance under VIVA LA LIBERTÀ!; the shake on CHI SIETE?; his laughing pop under RIDI!; the sky flooding the grid at PENTITI!; the faint last flicker in the returning dusk.

### THE BARBER OF SEVILLE (Italian, comedy)

- **Logline.** A barber promises he can fix everything and proves it by being everywhere at once.
- **Hero.** Figaro, a gold disc, baritone. We follow him: he's the busiest disc in the series, and only a hero's journey keeps his bustle from reading as noise.
- **Others.** Rosina, a sakura disc, glimpsed and matched. Bartolo, slow and late, is folded into the bulb field.
- **Grid.** 13 by 13, small and fast. Background black throughout.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `1 2 3 5 4 2 1`, rhythm `xxxxxxx-`, "Largo al factotum"'s tumbling scalar run over a vamp that never varies. C major, tempo 152, relentless — the breath is Bartolo's fragment landing painfully behind the beat. Figaro states it first at full patter speed; change voice as Bartolo manages only the fragment, slow and augmented, comically behind; Rosina sings it transposed up, bright; diminish as Figaro's own restatements get faster each time. Room dry and bright throughout, one brief cavernous echo when Bartolo finally lands a note in time, then yanked dry again.
- **Kawaii.** Figaro has a little double-hop skip on every landing, his bustle made visible, with a rapid double-pluck to match.

**I. Figaro!** (intro, about 4 s)
- Light: gold and dusk.
- Soloist: Figaro's double-hop, quick.
- Mass: chase — a short run of gold cells travels the outer ring at speed.
- Cue: FIGARO!

**II. Patter.** (build)
- Light: gold and bulb.
- Soloist: Figaro bounces, keeping the pace exact.
- Mass: chase continues, a beat faster.
- Cue: none.

**III. Lindoro.**
- Light: gold and sakura.
- Soloist: Figaro grows, pleased with his plot.
- Mass: scan — one bright sakura cell sweeps back and forth along a row, threading past his chase.
- Cue: LINDORO!

**IV. Zitto.** (the freeze)
- Light: gold and bulb.
- Soloist: Figaro pops still, mid-hop, caught.
- Mass: none — the sequencer cut dead.
- Cue: ZITTO!

**V. Bravo! Factotum!**
- Light: gold and bulb.
- Soloist: Figaro bounces at top speed, delighted.
- Mass: chase at its fastest, the outer ring at maximum pace.
- Cue: BRAVO! then FACTOTUM!

**VI. Never stopped.** (outro, about 5 s, no fade)
- Light: gold and dusk, the grid cutting instantly to its resting state.
- Soloist: Figaro's double-hop once more, already moving.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.
- **Turnaround.** The grid cuts dead then relights on Figaro's chase already at its entrance position — the sequencer never actually stopped.

**Running time.** About 55 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** Figaro's double-hop under FIGARO!; the chase quickening through Patter; the scan threading past him at LINDORO!; the freeze on ZITTO!; the chase at full speed under BRAVO!/FACTOTUM!; the instant cut back to the resting grid.

### TURANDOT (Italian, tragedy turning to dawn)

- **Logline.** A single voice refuses to sleep until dawn proves it right.
- **Hero.** Calaf, a gold disc, tenor. We follow him: his patience is the whole opera's shape, a long hold that finally releases.
- **Others.** Turandot, a sky disc: cold, fixed, distant. Liù is a brief sakura flicker, fading.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background black throughout, lightening only at the bloom before returning.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `1 2 3 4 5 7+`, rhythm `x.x.x.x.x...x---`, "Nessun dorma"'s stepwise climb capped by one late high note, acceptance not victory. Ab major, tempo 66, breathing hardest of any card, every phrase easing late into its final note. Calaf sings it first, alone, unhurried; Liù answers a fragment, just the climb, quietly, before fading; augment as dawn nears, the whole motif doubling in length; change voice at the climax as Turandot sings Calaf's motif back to him, the cold voice taking the warm tune. Room almost fully closed at first, opening gradually and continuously — the room itself performs the dawn.
- **Kawaii.** Calaf carries a tiny star riding his disc, a small wish made visible. His heartbeat quickens the nearer dawn comes, and the chime that goes with it rings a little longer each time.

**I. Nessun dorma.** (intro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and dusk.
- Soloist: Calaf pulses low and steady, alone.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: NESSUN DORMA.

**II. Liù's fragment.**
- Light: gold, sakura brief at one corner.
- Soloist: Calaf holds steady, unhurried.
- Mass: sparkle — a brief shimmer at Liù's corner, fading.
- Cue: none.

**III. The climb.**
- Light: gold and sky, Turandot fixed and cold.
- Soloist: Calaf grows steadily, rising.
- Mass: fillCentre — rings light outward from the grid's centre as he climbs.
- Cue: DILEGUA.

**IV. The long wait.**
- Light: gold and sky.
- Soloist: Calaf holds, a sustained pulse, patient.
- Mass: fillCentre continues, the rings reaching further out — the long hold building.
- Cue: none.

**V. Vincerò!** (dawn, the bloom)
- Light: gold floods; sky recedes.
- Soloist: Calaf grows to fill more of the grid, the hold finally releasing.
- Mass: fillCentre completes, the rings flooding outward to the grid's edge, held.
- Cue: VINCERÒ!

**VI. Dawn settles.** (outro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and sky fade together to dusk, matching the intro.
- Soloist: one small pulse before the loop.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: none.
- **Turnaround.** The grid settles into the same dusk pinprick that opened it, Calaf's pulse about to begin again — dawn becoming the night before the next dawn.

**Running time.** About 65 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** Calaf's slow pulse alone; Liù's fading sparkle at the corner; the fillCentre rings beginning under DILEGUA; the long hold building further out; the rings flooding the grid at VINCERÒ!; the fade back to the pinprick dusk.
