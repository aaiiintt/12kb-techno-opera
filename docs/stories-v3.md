# Story cards v3

Written against the third visual guide. Each card follows one hero disc through the opera's own acts. Per act: one lighting state, one mass move, one soloist move, and the sounds that light them. Lights are named from the palette; nothing else. Music blocks carry over from v2 unchanged where noted.

---

### CARMEN (French, tragedy)

- **Logline.** A soldier's heart is taken by a woman who will not be kept, and he cannot let her go.
- **Hero.** José, a gold disc, tenor. We follow him.
- **Others.** Carmen, a coral disc, soprano: she moves freely, never on the grid's straight lines. The crowd is the field in bulb and coral.
- **Grid.** 9 by 9. Background black throughout; one cut to coral at the knife.
- **Music (from v2, unchanged).** Motif `5 4 3 2 1`, rhythm `x-x.x-x.`, the Habanera's sinking line over a tonic pedal. D Phrygian, tempo 72, ritardando into the last chord. Carmen sings first; José answers in canon; José takes her motif when he is lost to her; retrograde when he cannot undo it; augment on the stab. Room bright and wide, closing as the story tightens, near-silent for the knife.
- **Kawaii.** José has a heartbeat you can see: his disc pulses on a soft kick. Carmen's flower is a single sparkle that lands on him. When he is in love his disc is a little bigger.

**I. The guard.** (intro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and dusk.
- Soloist: José at the centre, lit only by his heartbeat, slow.
- Mass: none. The field is dusk.
- Cue: none.

**II. The flower.** (bars 1 to 4)
- Light: coral enters. Gold, coral.
- Soloist: Carmen hops in from the left edge on the beat, singing the motif; her disc follows every note. She circles José, one cell away, never touching.
- Mass: a slow scan in dusk-coral along the bottom row, one cell per beat, the arp's pedal made visible.
- End of act: she throws the flower. One sparkle arcs to José; his disc pops and grows a little. His heartbeat quickens.
- Cue: L'AMOUR.

**III. The crowd.** (bars 5 to 8)
- Light: bulb and coral. The crowd cheers for the toreador.
- Soloist: José answers her motif in canon, a beat behind. He shrinks a step each bar as the crowd grows.
- Mass: a chase in bulb around the grid's outer ring, faster each bar, lit by the arp and the hats.
- Cue: TORÉADOR!

**IV. Lost to her.** (bars 9 to 12)
- Light: coral only. Gold is gone from the field; only José is gold.
- Soloist: José sings Carmen's motif on his own tenor, the change of voice. His disc follows it.
- Mass: fillCentre in coral from Carmen's cell, ring by ring on each planed chord, until José is surrounded.
- Cue: JE T'AIME.

**V. Libre.** (bars 13 to 15)
- Light: coral and bulb.
- Soloist: Carmen breaks away, hopping to the far corner. The coral field follows her: every lit cell decays except those near her.
- Mass: an explode in coral from Carmen's corner on the crescendo, the one big burst of the piece.
- Cue: LIBRE.

**VI. The knife.** (bar 16)
- Light: black, then a cut to coral background on the stab.
- One full bar of silence: everything dusk, even José.
- The stab: a single flash on the dry chord, the background cuts to coral for one beat, closeUp on Carmen's disc as it burns out on the augmented retrograde.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: ADIEU.

**VII. Alone.** (outro, about 6 s)
- Light: gold and dusk. Background black.
- Soloist: José at the centre, where he began. The pedal note holds; his heartbeat returns, slower.
- Mass: none.
- Cue: CARMEN...
- **Turnaround.** The last state is the first: José alone at the centre in dusk, heartbeat slow. The loop is seamless.

**Running time.** About 60 seconds.

**Taste gate frames.** José's heartbeat alone; Carmen circling with the scan; the chase at its fastest; the coral fill surrounding José; the explode; the closeUp on the burning disc.
