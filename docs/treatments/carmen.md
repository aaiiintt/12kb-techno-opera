# Carmen: treatment

**Hero: Carmen**, not José. She's the one we root for: free, dangerous, doomed. A coral disc.
**José** is gold: devoted, then jealous. **Escamillo** is lemon: a comet. **Seville** is the grid in bulb white.
**Tune:** the Habanera, D minor, 72 bpm, over its dotted bass (dum, da-dum dum).
Opening phrase, *L'amour est un oiseau rebelle*: `1+ 7# 7 6# 6 5`, the chromatic slide from D down to A. Answer, *que nul ne peut apprivoiser*: `5 4 5 6 5 4 3`.
About 60 seconds.

**I. Seville.** (6 s) Heat. The whole screen breathes in dim bulb on the habanera bass, each downbeat lifting a column. In the middle of the crowd one cell is coral.

**II. L'amour.** (16 s) Carmen sings the slide. Every note lights a ring around her, one ring further out per semitone, so the chromatic descent becomes red rings spreading across the grid. The crowd sways: left half on "dum", right half on "da-dum". Cue: L'AMOUR.

**III. The flower.** (8 s) She throws it: one sakura spark arcs across the grid to a gold cell at the edge. José. His cell blooms, and a halo of gold rings starts to beat like a heart.

**IV. Toréador.** (8 s) Escamillo arrives as a lemon comet racing round the outside of the stage on the tune, transposed up. The crowd flashes in stripes. Carmen's rings turn lemon where he passes; José's gold halo shrinks. Cue: TORÉADOR!

**V. Jealousy.** (8 s) José sings her tune back to her, inverted and minor. His gold spreads row by row towards her, a wall eating the red. The room closes.

**VI. The knife.** (4 s) One bar of silence, everything dark. One beat of white across the whole screen. Every red ring collapses inward to Carmen, and she burns out cell by cell on the tune, slowed and backwards.

**VII. Libre.** (8 s) José alone in gold. Carmen's embers rise up the screen like petals and out through the top edge. The music box plays the slide once more. The embers become the warm bulb breathing of Seville, and it begins again.

**The unforgettable idea:** the Habanera's chromatic slide *is* the picture. Each semitone is a ring of red spreading out from her. You see the tune.
