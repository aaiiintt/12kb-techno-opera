Eight lights

Defined in OKLCH so that lightness is perceptual: half as bright looks half as bright in every hue. The face is oklch(L C H). Brightness is L alone. Chroma falls as a light dims, the way a bulb goes warm and grey before it goes out. Off is L 0.22 at almost no chroma.

Bigger is closer

One gold light at six sizes, all at the same L. The halo radius follows the size; its opacity follows L. A faint wide halo appears only on large discs. Nothing pulses on its own.

As a rainbow

Seven hues at equal L and C, which is why they sit together as a family.

Light is the voice

The hero disc's L is driven by the tenor's actual output, sampled from the engine's master bus every frame. Attack, vibrato, release: whatever the oscillator does, the light does. Nothing is animated.
