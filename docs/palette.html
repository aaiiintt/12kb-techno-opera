Eight lights

Flat discs on black, milky rather than pure. Each at five brightness steps. Bulbs behind a perfect diffuser. Off is not a colour; it is any light at its lowest step in dusk.

Bigger is closer

The same gold light at six sizes. The tight halo scales with the disc. A second, wide, soft halo appears only as the disc gets big, so a large disc feels near and a small one feels far. The largest one is sounding a note: the halo blooms and settles.

As a rainbow

Seven hues in wheel order. Enough for a rainbow, few enough to stay a family.

On the grid

Nine by nine in dusk. A gold hero, sounding. A sakura beloved beside it.
