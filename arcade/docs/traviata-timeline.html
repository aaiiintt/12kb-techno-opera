La traviata, beat by beat

The same grammar as Carmen: five games, one toy, seven scenes, the story told between the games in plain words. Nothing here is built yet. Approve it or change it and it gets built on the same runner.

The grammar

The same as Carmen's approved timeline: WATCH beats are cutscenes (bars in, one typed caption, no fuse); PLAY beats have the fuse, a verb, an instruction line and an animated icon; a TOY has no fuse and no verdict. Losing never changes the story: both outcomes end on one tableau and the second caption line states the same fact. The last thing the player does is a game; the ending is watched. Five games, one toy, under two minutes.

Four inputs, four pictograms

Only these four, so a player learns them in the first opera and never again. Touch and mouse are the same gesture. The keyboard is a courtesy, shown small on desktop.

  3. 0:088 s

    TOAST! PLAY · tap

    Do
    TAP TO CLINK ON THE BEAT Two glasses swing toward each other; a gold square closes on the meeting point. Tap on the downbeat. Three clinks.
    Win
    The glasses ring, three times.
    Lose
    They miss. A glass breaks. She laughs.
    Then
    Both: a cough, her hand to her mouth, Alfredo's hand on her arm. SHE COUGHS. SHE LAUGHS IT OFF.
    SHE LEAVES PARIS TO LIVE WITH ALFREDO.
  4. 0:2110 s

    SNIP! TOY · no fuse, no verdict

    See
    A garden in the country. Camellias on a bush, a basket, a bee wandering, petals drifting.
    Do
    DRAG TO PICK THE FLOWERS Slide the scissors along the bush; each blossom it meets drops into the basket with a note. No way to fail.
    Hear
    "Un dì, felice" would go here but is not verified; a soft waltz in the key instead.
    Caption
    HAPPY MONTHS IN THE COUNTRY. At the end.
  5. 0:346 s

    A man in grey WATCH

    See
    The garden gate. A man in a grey coat and a top hat, still. Violetta sets the basket down.
    Hear
    A low held note, the waltz stopping.
    Caption
    ALFREDO'S FATHER, GERMONT.
    THE SCANDAL WILL RUIN HIS DAUGHTER'S WEDDING.
    HE ASKS VIOLETTA TO LEAVE ALFREDO. FOR GOOD.

    The ask, and the reason for it, so the next game is not a puzzle.
  6. 0:428 s

    RENOUNCE! PLAY · hold

    Do
    HOLD TO SIGN THE LETTER A big letter on the table, its outline pulsing. Hold: the signature draws itself. Let go and the ink runs back.
    Win
    She signs it.
    Lose
    Her hand shakes. Germont signs it for her.
    Then
    Both: the letter signed, Germont gone, Violetta alone. SHE WILL LEAVE ALFREDO.
    SHE CANNOT TELL HIM WHY.
    Hear
    "Pura siccome un angelo" is not verified; a slow bass line in the key.
  7. 0:556 s

    Flora's party WATCH

    See
    Paris again, another salon, a card table. Violetta on the Baron's arm, not looking at Alfredo. Alfredo winning, coins stacking, his face set.
    Hear
    A gambling figure, quick and thin.
    Caption
    PARIS. FLORA'S PARTY.
    ALFREDO THINKS SHE LEFT HIM FOR THE BARON.
    HE WINS AT CARDS. HE WANTS TO PAY HER BACK.
    Story
    What he believes, wrongly, and what he is about to do with it.
  8. 1:037 s

    FLING! PLAY · tap tap tap

    Do
    TAP TAP TAP TO THROW THE COINS A purse in his hand. Each tap a coin, arcing at her feet. Twelve coins.
    Win
    Every coin at her feet. Paris gasps.
    Lose
    The purse splits. The gold rolls to her feet anyway.
    Then
    Both: gold on the floor round her, the guests turned away from him. HE HAS SHAMED HER IN FRONT OF PARIS.
    HIS FATHER ARRIVES AND SHAMES HIM.
  9. 1:156 s

    The sickroom WATCH

    See
    Months later. A bare room, a bed, one candle. Violetta small in the bed. A letter on the blanket.
    Hear
    "Addio, del passato", verified, on the soprano, thin.
    Caption
    MONTHS LATER. VIOLETTA IS DYING.
    A LETTER FROM GERMONT. SHE READS IT AGAIN.
    Story
    Time has passed; the ending is coming; the letter is the last hope.
  10. 1:238 s

    READ! PLAY · hold

    Do
    HOLD TO SHIELD THE FLAME Her hand and the candle. Draughts come as streaks; while held, her hand cups the flame. The letter's lines light up as she reads.
    Win
    She reads it to the end.
    Lose
    The candle dies. She knows it by heart.
    Then
    Both: the letter to her chest. GERMONT HAS TOLD ALFREDO THE TRUTH. HE IS COMING.
    È TARDI ! beside Violetta (too late).
  11. 1:365 s

    Alfredo WATCH

    See
    The door. Alfredo runs in. She sits up. They hold each other. Colour comes back into the room for a moment.
    Hear
    A warm rise, the Brindisi bass under it, slow.
    Caption
    ALFREDO. THEY WILL LEAVE PARIS TOGETHER, HE SAYS.
    SHE FEELS STRONG AGAIN. FOR A MOMENT.
    Story
    The reunion, and the false dawn named as one.
  12. 1:436 s

    GIVE! PLAY · drag

    Do
    DRAG THE PORTRAIT TO HIS HAND Violetta sitting up with a small framed picture. Alfredo's open hand a little way off, bobbing. Slide it across.
    Win
    She puts it in his hand.
    Lose
    It slips. He picks it up.
    Then
    Both: the portrait in his hand, Germont in the doorway. SHE GIVES HIM HER PORTRAIT.
    FOR THE GIRL HE WILL MARRY ONE DAY.
  13. 1:548 s

    The end WATCH

    See
    She stands. White camellia petals begin to fall over the room. She says the pain has gone. She falls. Alfredo catches her. Two beats of stillness.
    Hear
    "Addio, del passato" once more, the room opening, then silence.
    Caption
    SHE SAYS THE PAIN HAS GONE.
    SHE DIES.

    Plain, so nobody wonders. The petals do the feeling.

Notes

  • 1READ becomes a hold (shield the flame while held) and GIVE a drag, so nothing needs the arrow keys.
  • 2The toy stays in the country, early, where the opera is tender; the end is watched with the petals, as Carmen's is. Two petal endings in the series is a rhyme, not a repeat: hers are white.
  • 3Germont's ask is given its reason in a caption, or RENOUNCE reads as her signing something for no reason.
  • 4The Brindisi and Addio del passato are verified. Un dì felice, Pura siccome un angelo and the gambling figure are filler in the key, not quotes, until they are checked.
  • 5It runs two or three seconds over the ceiling. Trim: the sickroom to five seconds and GIVE's outcome to three.
