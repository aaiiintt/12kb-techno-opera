Il barbiere, beat by beat

The same grammar as Carmen: five games, one toy, seven scenes, the story told between the games in plain words. Nothing here is built yet. Approve it or change it and it gets built on the same runner.

The grammar

The same as Carmen's approved timeline: WATCH beats are cutscenes (bars in, one typed caption, no fuse); PLAY beats have the fuse, a verb, an instruction line and an animated icon; a TOY has no fuse and no verdict. Losing never changes the story: both outcomes end on one tableau and the second caption line states the same fact. The last thing the player does is a game; the ending is watched. Five games, one toy, under two minutes.

Four inputs, four pictograms

Only these four, so a player learns them in the first opera and never again. Touch and mouse are the same gesture. The keyboard is a courtesy, shown small on desktop.

The timeline

Fourteen beats, about two minutes. Five to play, seven to watch, one to feel.

  1. 0:00title

    IL BARBIERE title card

    See
    Seville at dawn, a balcony, a shuttered window. The title.
    Hear
    A guitar arpeggio, waiting.
    Do
    TAP TO BEGIN The only instruction on the card.
  2. 0:035 s

    Under the balcony WATCH

    See
    Dawn. Count Almaviva under Rosina's balcony with a guitar; a musician or two behind him. The shutters closed.
    Hear
    "Ecco ridente in cielo", verified.
    Caption
    SEVILLE. DAWN. A BALCONY.
    COUNT ALMAVIVA LOVES ROSINA. SHE HAS NEVER SEEN HIM.
    Story
    Who he is, who she is, and that she does not know him yet.
  3. 0:088 s

    SERENADE! PLAY · tap

    Do
    TAP TO STRUM ON THE BEAT A big note over his head with a ring closing on it each beat. Tap when the ring meets the note: six strums. The window is the only lit thing.
    Win
    The shutters open. A note flutters down.
    Lose
    He strums off the beat. The note is thrown and hits his head.
    Then
    Both: he has her note in his hand. SHE DROPS HIM A NOTE.
    HE SAYS HE IS LINDORO, A POOR STUDENT. A LIE.
  4. 0:215 s

    Figaro WATCH

    See
    Figaro strides in with his guitar and his shop sign. The Count grabs him. Through the window, Bartolo's silhouette locks a door.
    Hear
    "Largo al factotum", verified.
    Caption
    FIGARO. THE BARBER. HE FIXES EVERYTHING, FOR A FEE.
    DR BARTOLO KEEPS ROSINA LOCKED UP. HE MEANS TO MARRY HER HIMSELF.
    Story
    The fixer and the obstacle, in one breath.
  5. 0:288 s

    SLIP! PLAY · drag

    Do
    DRAG THE LETTER UNDER THE DOOR Rosina's room. A letter at her feet, a line of light under the door across the room, Bartolo in his chair with a newspaper. Drag the letter along the floor. His paper trembles a beat before he looks up: moving while he looks is being caught.
    Win
    Under the door, into the light.
    Lose
    AH-HA! He catches her. She kicks it under the door anyway.
    Then
    Both: the letter is gone, Bartolo grumbling. THE LETTER IS ON ITS WAY.
    ROSINA WANTS LINDORO TOO.
    Hear
    "Una voce poco fa", verified.
  6. 0:415 s

    A drunk soldier WATCH

    See
    Bartolo's front door. The Count reels up in a soldier's coat, a bottle in one hand and a paper in the other. Bartolo in the doorway, arms folded.
    Hear
    A march that stumbles.
    Caption
    THE COUNT COMES BACK IN DISGUISE: A DRUNK SOLDIER.
    HIS PAPERS SAY HE MAY SLEEP HERE. BARTOLO SAYS NO.
    Story
    Disguise one, and why it gets him through the door.
  7. 0:487 s

    STAGGER! PLAY · tap

    Do
    TAP TO STEADY HIM He crosses the hall toward Bartolo, tipping left or right; an arrow glows on the side he is falling to. Tap while it glows to right him. Four tips.
    Win
    He arrives upright and slaps the billet on Bartolo's chest.
    Lose
    He falls. The billet lands on Bartolo's head anyway.
    Then
    Both: Bartolo frozen with the paper, the Count and Rosina swapping a glance. BARTOLO FREEZES.
    THE LOVERS SWAP NOTES UNDER HIS NOSE.
  8. 1:005 s

    The music lesson WATCH

    See
    The music room. A harpsichord. The Count again, now in a black gown and spectacles. Bartolo settles into a chair to listen.
    Hear
    A polite scale, then a yawn.
    Caption
    DISGUISE TWO: DON ALONSO, MUSIC TEACHER.
    THE LESSON IS A COVER. THEY PLAN TO ELOPE TONIGHT.
    Story
    Disguise two, and the plan said out loud.
  9. 1:078 s

    TUNE! PLAY · tap

    Do
    TAP THE GOLD KEY The keyboard. One key at a time turns gold; tap it before it fades. Each right note sends Bartolo's head a little lower.
    Win
    Bartolo snores.
    Lose
    A wrong note. Bartolo leaps up, then dozes off anyway.
    Then
    Both: Bartolo asleep in the chair, the lovers whispering. BARTOLO SNORES.
    MIDNIGHT, THE BALCONY. THAT IS THE PLAN.
  10. 1:2010 s

    SHAVE! TOY · no fuse, no verdict

    See
    Figaro at the chair with the brush. Bartolo half asleep. Foam.
    Do
    DRAG TO LATHER Slide the brush over him: foam piles up wherever it goes, each pass a note of the Largo. Foam to the wig. No way to fail; it just gets sillier.
    Hear
    "Largo al factotum" note by note under the brush, verified.
    Caption
    FIGARO POCKETS THE BALCONY KEY. At the end, as the foam settles.
  11. 1:336 s

    Midnight WATCH

    See
    A storm. Lightning. A ladder against the balcony. Figaro and the Count climb in through the window. Rosina turns her back on them.
    Hear
    Thunder, a tremolo, then quiet.
    Caption
    MIDNIGHT. A STORM. A LADDER.
    BARTOLO HAS TOLD ROSINA THAT LINDORO IS A FRAUD.
    HE SHOWS HER: LINDORO IS THE COUNT. SHE SAYS YES.
    Story
    The last obstacle and the reveal, in three lines, because it has to happen fast.
  12. 1:418 s

    WED! PLAY · tap tap tap

    Do
    TAP TAP TAP TO CLIMB DOWN Rosina on the ladder. Each tap a rung. Bartolo's footsteps get louder along the top: he is coming back with the notary.
    Win
    Down the ladder into the Count's arms.
    Lose
    Bartolo pulls the ladder away. She jumps. Caught anyway.
    Then
    Both: the notary at the door, the couple hand in hand, Bartolo arriving too late. THE NOTARY MARRIES THEM ON THE SPOT.
    BARTOLO KEEPS THE DOWRY. EVERYONE IS HAPPY.

Notes

  • 1STAGGER and WED lose their arrow keys: steadying is a timed tap on a glowing arrow, climbing is a mash. Nothing in the opera needs left and right any more.
  • 2The two disguises are each said in a caption before their game, so the same man in three coats reads as a plan, not a bug.
  • 3SHAVE stays the toy and moves nothing forward but the key, which is the joke: the whole plot turns on a barber pocketing a key while lathering.
  • 4Ecco ridente, Una voce and the Largo are verified. The march, the scale and the storm are filler in the key and are not claimed as quotes.
  • 5It runs right at the two-minute ceiling. If it must give, the bill beat folds into WED's outcome (the tag appears there) and Midnight drops to five seconds.
